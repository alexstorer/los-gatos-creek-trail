# Los Gatos Creek Trail Explorer

An interactive map, timeline and cost tracker for the **Los Gatos Creek Trail**, from Lexington Dam through Los Gatos and Campbell to downtown San José. A sister site to the [Coyote Creek Trail Explorer](https://alexstorer.github.io/coyote-creek-trail/).

**Live site:** https://alexstorer.github.io/los-gatos-creek-trail/

## What's inside

- **Map**: every reach, colored by status (open paved, open gravel, in design, planned gap). San José and Campbell come from the City of San José's Trail Reach GIS layer; the Los Gatos section comes from OpenStreetMap.
- **Timeline**: 61 dated events from 1935 to 2028. They cover the dams and ponds, master plans, openings, the Willow Glen trestle fight, Google's Downtown West, floods and encampment sweeps, and the planned Hwy 17 crossings. Each one links to its source. As you scroll, the map flies to where each event happened and redraws the trail as it stood on that date.
- **Costs**: the recent projects with published costs (Three Creeks bridge, Hwy 9 trailhead connector, Pruneyard spur) and cost per mile against the City's and VTA's estimates for the downtown gaps. It also includes delivery scorecards, San José's 1985 estimate phase by phase, and a calculator for closing the remaining ~1.5 miles of gaps.
- **Documents**: 61 master plans, design plans, maps, CEQA filings and council reports, each pinned to the stretch of trail it covers. Also an atlas of 19 figures (`img/schematics/`) cut from them, ordered north to south.
- **News**: 47 local articles from 2007 to 2026.
- **Photos**: 25 geotagged, openly licensed images from Wikimedia Commons, pinned on the map and attached to timeline events.

## Data sources

- **Trail geometry:** stitched together by `scripts/update_reaches.py`. Raw copies are in `data/source/`.
  - [City of San José Trail Reach dataset](https://data.sanjoseca.gov/dataset/trail-reach) (ArcGIS layer `OPN_OpenDataService/MapServer/538`, features named "Los Gatos Creek"). San José reaches keep their City IDs (`LGC01`–`LGC07`). The long unnamed county feature is cut at road crossings into `CAM1`, `CAM2`, `LGCP` and `VAS1`.
  - [OpenStreetMap relation 5335571](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/5335571) (ODbL) for Vasona Lake to Lexington Dam, cut at Hwy 9 and Main St into `LG1`–`LG3`. A reach is "Open (gravel)" when most of its OSM ways are tagged unpaved.
- **Costs and dates:** San José Council memos and annual trail reports (Legistar), Los Gatos and Campbell staff reports, County Parks plans, Valley Water and VTA documents, and local news. Each one is cited inline in `data/data.js`.
- **Location links:** `data/geo.js` connects events, documents and plan sheets to reaches, and records the year each reach opened. Years marked `approx` are guesses from the earliest record. That covers all of Campbell and Los Gatos, which date from roughly the late 1970s.
- **Basemap:** Esri Canvas tiles with © OpenStreetMap contributors.

## Caveats

- The gap-closure cost is an extrapolation, **not** an official estimate. The Meridian–Lincoln gap has never been designed or priced.
- Official trail lengths disagree (9, 9.5, 9.7, 11.9 and 14 miles), mostly because of where the north end is counted. This site measures 12.4 mapped mainline miles.

## Updating the site

Expect to update the site **at least once a year, after San José's spring Trail Network annual report**, and whenever one of the open items below resolves. The page shows the date of the last check, which comes from `SITE_UPDATED` in `data/data.js`.

### Where everything lives

| What | File | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Trail geometry, status, length | `data/reaches.geojson`, `data/reaches.js` | Generated by `scripts/update_reaches.py`. Don't edit by hand. |
| Timeline events | `TIMELINE` in `data/data.js` | `d` is the date (`YYYY`, `YYYY-MM` or `YYYY-MM-DD`) and also serves as the key everywhere else. Mark expected events `future: true`. |
| Where each event happened, plus its photos and plan sheets | `EVENT_GEO` in `data/geo.js` | Keyed by the event's `d`. Use `w` for a place label when there's a point but no reach. |
| Completed / schedule / budget scorecards | `OUTCOMES` in `data/data.js` | Status is one of `good`, `warn`, `bad`, `unk` or `pend`. |
| Project costs, per-mile estimates, calculator presets | `PROJECTS`, `PM_EST`, `PRESETS` in `data/data.js` | |
| Delivery table and the 1985 estimate | `DELIVERY` and `OFFICIAL_EST` in `data/data.js` | |
| Remaining gaps (cost calculator) | `GAPS` in `data/data.js` | |
| Headline numbers | `STATS` in `data/data.js`; intro copy in `index.html` | "10.2 mi", "$9.3M" and "$13.9M" are typed in by hand. |
| Year each reach opened (timeline map) | `BUILT` in `data/geo.js` | |
| Reach popups, side routes, owners | `REACH_NOTES`, `PARENT`, `SIDE_KIND`, `OWNERS` in `data/data.js` | |
| Documents, with their locations and dates | `DOCS` in `data/data.js`; `DOC_GEO` and `DOC_DATES` in `data/geo.js` | All keyed by the exact document title. |
| Plan-sheet atlas | `SCHEMATICS` and `SCH_GROUPS` in `data/geo.js`; images in `img/schematics/` | Images are made with `scripts/make_figure.py`. |
| News, photos, quotes | `NEWS`, `PHOTOS`, `QUOTES` in `data/data.js` | Quotes must be verbatim from the cited article. |

### Update checklist

1. **Refresh the trail data.** `python3 scripts/update_reaches.py` (or `--offline` to rebuild from `data/source/`). It prints every new, removed or changed reach. When a reach opens, add it to `BUILT`, remove it from `GAPS`, add a timeline event and recheck the headline mileage.
2. **Check the sources** for anything new since `SITE_UPDATED`:
   - **San José:** search [Legistar](https://sanjose.legistar.com/) for "Los Gatos Creek". The T&E Committee's *Bike Plan and Trail Network Annual Report* comes out each March (latest: `SRC.te2026`).
   - **Los Gatos:** the [Hwy 17 bike/ped overcrossing](https://www.losgatosca.gov/2556/Hwy-17-Bicycle-Pedestrian-Overcrossing) page and council agendas.
   - **Campbell:** council agendas (Pruneyard spur closeout; Hamilton Ave/SR 17 crossing).
   - **Midpen / VTA:** the [Hwy 17 wildlife and trail crossing](https://ridgetrail.org/highway17/).
   - **County Parks:** [closures and alerts](https://parks.santaclaracounty.gov/closures-and-alerts).
   - **Google Downtown West:** any restart, which would trigger the creek-trail segments.
   - **News:** San José Spotlight, the Los Gatan, San José Inside, Mercury News, KQED, KTVU.
3. **Close out open items** whose dates have passed: turn `future: true` events into real ones, and change `pend` scorecards to outcomes.
4. **Add documents and plan sheets.** `python3 scripts/make_figure.py path/to/file.pdf PAGE key [--crop x0 y0 x1 y1]`, then add the result to `SCHEMATICS` and `SCH_GROUPS`.
5. **Validate.** `node scripts/check_data.js` must print `OK`.
6. **Look at it.** `python3 -m http.server`, then check the intro, timeline, map popups and costs on desktop and phone widths.
7. **Stamp and publish.** `python3 scripts/stamp.py`, then `git commit -am "Update: <what changed>" && git push`.

### Open items to watch (as of Nov 20, 2026)

- **Meridian–Lincoln gap:** whether the City starts any design, and which gap VTA's $2.59M Measure B "gap closure" money is for.
- **San Carlos St undercrossing (LGC02):** at 90% design since 2019; construction unfunded.
- **Downtown West:** the creek-trail segments and park wait on Google restarting.
- **Hwy 17 crossings:** the Blossom Hill Rd bike bridge ($26.6M; grant decisions in 2027) and the wildlife and trail crossing near Lexington (construction planned mid-2027).
- **Pruneyard spur:** confirm the March 2026 acceptance date and final cost.
- **Opening years** for the Campbell, County and Los Gatos sections, and for Leigh–Bascom and San Carlos–Auzerais in San José. These need library or newspaper archives (SJPL California Room, Los Gatos Library, CDNC).

## Running locally

This is a static site with no build step:

```
python3 -m http.server
```

Then open http://localhost:8000.
